## Selected AI Research Projects

### [Human–AI Consciousness Lab](https://github.com/durganihantri/human-ai-consciousness-lab)

**Independent Open Research Initiative | Active Development**

An interdisciplinary research initiative investigating how conversational AI influences human perceptions of consciousness, agency, subjective experience, and psychological identity.

Our first research track, **Experiment 01A**, examines the effects of first-person self-reference in fictional AI dialogues on human attribution judgments.

The project currently includes an operational technical prototype, open research documentation, automated tests, and public methodological review tasks.

**Current stage:** Research pre-release. Experimental stimuli and measures have not yet undergone independent validation. No human-participant study or empirical findings are claimed.

[Explore the Research Repository](https://github.com/durganihantri/human-ai-consciousness-lab) | [View the Technical Demo](https://durganihantri.github.io/human-ai-consciousness-lab/experiments/01a-first-person/offline-prototype/)

### [AI Child Behavior Assessment](https://github.com/durganihantri/AI-Child-Behavior-Assesment)

An exploratory multimodal AI project examining facial and speech signals in child behavior research.

### [AI Reading Engagement](https://github.com/durganihantri/AI-Reading-Engagement)

An AI-assisted research prototype exploring reading motivation, emotional engagement, and adaptive learning experiences.

### [MicroAggression Insight Tool](https://github.com/durganihantri/MicroAggression-Insight-Tool)

An NLP-based prototype exploring microaggression categories, language patterns, and sentiment analysis.

*These projects are exploratory research prototypes and are not validated clinical diagnostic tools.*

My interdisciplinary approach seeks to build meaningful dialogue between psychological science, artificial intelligence, consciousness studies, and ancient Indian philosophical traditions.

Rather than treating spiritual traditions and scientific research as interchangeable forms of evidence, I am interested in examining where their questions, perspectives, and approaches to human experience intersect—and where they differ.

My broader aim is to contribute to a deeper understanding of consciousness, human identity, emotional experience, and meaningful personal transformation.
